Report vermin or pests from a waste facility, industrial site or farm in England

! This start page is in progress. Content is not complete and will change.

Use this service to report vermin or pests from waste processing, large industrial sites and farming activities in England.

This includes vermin or pests from:

  • waste sites, for example landfills or recycling centres
  • large industrial sites or factories
  • agricultural sites or activities
Do not use this service to report vermin or pests from neighbours or small local businesses. Contact your local council's environmental health team for help.

Before you start

You'll need to know:

  • where the vermin or pests are coming from, for example the name of the site or business responsible
  • where you noticed them
  • when you first noticed them

You only need to give your personal details if you're happy to be contacted for more information.

You'll need to give an email address if you want to either:

  • include any photos
  • get a reference number

After you make your report

The Environment Agency will review your report and decide if any action is needed.

You will not get updates on your report.

Other environmental problems

How you report other problems affecting the environment depends on what it is and where it's happening. You may have to report it to:

  • the Environment Agency
  • your local council
  • your utilities company
