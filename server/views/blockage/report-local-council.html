Report the blockage directly

It's quicker to report blockages in some watercourses or systems directly to your council, utility company or the property owner.

Public sewers and water mains

Contact your local water companyto report:

  • blocked sewers
  • leaking or burst water mains

Minor rivers and streams, or drains

Contact your local council to report:

  • blockages in minor rivers, brooks or streams
  • blocked public drains

Private watercourses and sewage systems

Contact the property owner if you know who they are, otherwise contact your local council.
