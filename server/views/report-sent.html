What happens next

The Environment Agency will look into the problem. This involves:

  • checking for similar reports in the area
  • deciding how much danger to the environment there is
  • deciding how best to deal with the problem

We may:

  • send people to investigate
  • contact any people or organisations involved
  • pass details of the problem to local teams or other organisations

If you gave an email address, check your email for:

  • confirmation of your report and a reference number
  • how to send us photos to add to your report, if you said you had any and we need to see them

Otherwise, we will only contact you if we need more information about the problem - you do not need to call us.
