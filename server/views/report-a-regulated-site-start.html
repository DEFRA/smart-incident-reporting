This includes smells from:

  • rubbish tips
  • recycling centres
  • industrial food processing
  • livestock management, such as pig or poultry farming
There's a different way to report a smell in Scotland, report a smell in Wales or report an environmental problem in Northern Ireland.

Report other types of smells

You cannot use this service to report smells from:

  • neighbours
  • domestic rubbish
  • small or local businesses such as restaurants

You'll need to contact your local council and ask for their environmental health department.

After you make your report

The Environment Agency will review your report and decide if any action is needed.

You will not get updates on your report.

Other environmental problems

How you report other problems affecting the environment depends on what it is and where it's happening. You may have to report it to:

  • the Environment Agency
  • your local council
  • your utilities company
