Use this service to report blockage in a river in England.Blockages can include:

  • fallen trees or other vegetation
  • vehicles
  • a build-up of material such as stone or soil, or rubbish
  • someone deliberately blocking the river
There's a different way to report a blockage to a river in Wales, blockage to a river in Scotland or blockage to a river in Northern Ireland.

Before you start

You'll need to know the location of the blockage.

You only need to give your personal details if you're happy to be contacted for more information.

If you want to send photos, you'll need to give an email address.

Do not use this service to report blockages to other watercourses, such as streams or canals. Contact your local council instead.

After you make your report

The Environment Agency will review your report and decide if any action is needed.

You will not get updates on your report.

Report other problems with your environment

How you report other environmental problems depends on the type of issue and where it is. You may have to report it to:

  • the Environment Agency
  • your local council
  • your utilities company
